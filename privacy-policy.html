Privacy Policy

Business Invoice Pro · Last updated: September 2, 2026

Package: com.businessinvoicepro

1. Overview

Business Invoice Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

The App stores the following information locally on your device: business profile details, customer records, product catalog, invoices, quotations, logos, signatures, and preferences such as theme and currency. We do not operate a backend server that collects this business data.

3. Local Data Storage

All business data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. PDF documents and uploaded logos are saved to your device storage. Uninstalling the App may permanently delete stored data unless you have created your own backups.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may collect and process device information (such as advertising ID), IP address, and interaction data to show ads, measure performance, and fight fraud/abuse, according to Google’s policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

5. Permissions

The App may request:

  • Internet access — to load advertisements
  • Photo / media access — to upload a business logo
  • Storage access (where required by Android version) — to save and share PDF files

These permissions are used only for the stated features.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your business invoice/customer data. Data leaves your device only when:

  • You explicitly share a PDF (WhatsApp, email, etc.)
  • AdMob / Google advertising services process ad-related device data

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

You can clear app data or uninstall the App to remove local business information. Device advertising preferences can usually be managed in Android settings.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
